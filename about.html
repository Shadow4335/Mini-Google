How it works

Two ways to rank the same six pages: count the words, or follow the links. One is easy to cheat. The other is not.

Keyword ranking

Counts how many times the query words appear in a page. More repeats means a higher score.

Simple, fast, and trivially gamed.

PageRank

Pages that match are ordered by how important the pages linking to them are:

PR(p) = (1−d)/N + d·Σ PR(q)/out(q)

with d = 0.85, computed once for the whole graph.

Why keyword stuffing fails. pizza-pizza-pizza.com repeats “pizza” ten times, so it wins the left pane for the query “pizza”. But nobody links to it, so its PageRank is the lowest and it lands last in the right pane. Repeating words is easy; earning links from other pages is not.
